What Walkaway does

With your authorization, Walkaway operates in your browser, on accounts you are signed into, to go through a subscription service's cancellation flow far enough to accept a retention discount, and then stop. You direct it to do this by clicking to start a run. It does not cancel subscriptions and is designed so that it cannot press a final cancellation confirmation.
