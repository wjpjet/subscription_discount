Privacy Policy

Walkaway browser extension and website · Effective September 15, 2026 · walkaway home

Walkaway does one thing: it finds the subscriptions you're signed into and goes through their cancellation flows on your behalf to accept the loyalty discount a company offers to keep you, without cancelling anything. This policy explains what data that involves.

What we collect, and why

  • Names of websites you're signed into. With your permission, the extension looks at the names of cookies on your device to work out which sites you have an account with, and sends only those site names (for example hulu.com) to our service to identify subscription services. Cookie values, passwords, and your browsing history never leave your browser.
  • Text of the pages the extension works on. During a scan or a run, the extension sends the visible text and the list of buttons and links on a subscription service's account and cancellation pages to our AI service, so it can read your plan and price and decide what to click. This is the minimum needed for the extension to work. It is not stored beyond the run except in short-lived operational logs.
  • Email address and payment details. If you continue to checkout, Stripe collects your card and email. We receive your email (to send your summary and receipt) and payment identifiers; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Results. The outcome of each run (which service, before/after price, savings, the steps taken) is kept in your browser's extension storage and, if you paid, in our billing records to substantiate the charge.

How we use it

Only to provide the service described above: identifying your subscriptions, navigating cancellation flows to reach and accept a loyalty offer, verifying the new price, and charging the fee you agreed to. We do not use your data for advertising, do not build profiles, and do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Who else sees it

  • AI providers (Google Gemini and/or Anthropic Claude): receive site names and page text to make navigation decisions. They act as our processors for this purpose.
  • Stripe: processes payments and holds your card details under its own privacy policy.
  • Netlify: hosts our website and backend.

We transfer data to these providers only because it is necessary to provide the service, and to no one else except as required by law.

What we never do

  • Read or transmit cookie values, passwords, or browsing history.
  • Access sites you have not signed into or that are on your blocklist.
  • Press a final “confirm cancellation” — that action does not exist in the extension.
  • Have a person read your data, except with your consent for support, or as required for security or legal reasons.

Your controls

  • The extension asks for site access at the moment of your first scan; you can decline.
  • Restricted mode limits scans and runs to sites you list; the blocklist names sites it must never touch.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes its local data. Email us to delete billing records that are not required for accounting.

Retention

Page text sent for navigation is processed and discarded; operational logs are kept for at most 30 days. Billing records are kept as long as required for accounting and dispute handling.
