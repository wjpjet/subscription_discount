Many subscriptions have a second price

Get the leaving price. Keep the subscription.

Keeping you is cheaper than finding a new customer, so many subscriptions have a second, lower price that's offered only when you go to cancel. That's the leaving price. Walkaway AI goes and gets it for you, then stops. You keep the subscription. Your next bill is lower.

Why it works

We know where the offers are hidden.

Cancellation flows are built to keep you. Decoy offers, buried buttons, guilt screens, a survey before anything happens. We've been through a lot of them. Walkaway moves through each one the way that gets you the best offer, and since we only get paid when you save, it holds out for the best one rather than the first.

How it works

Scan, sit back, save.

1

Scan

Add the extension and click Scan. It finds the subscriptions you're signed into. Scanning is free.

2

Sit back

Walkaway starts each cancellation the way you would, gets through the surveys and the “are you sure” screens, and stops at the offer. Then it brings back what each service is willing to do. Nothing is accepted yet.

3

Save

Untick anything you'd rather keep as it is, then say yes. It accepts the rest and your bills come down. We charge 10% of what you actually saved, checked on your own billing page, and nothing if nothing was saved.

FAQ

Questions.

Will it ever actually cancel something?

No. “Confirm cancellation” isn't an action it has. Every screen is checked before any click, and on anything unclear it backs out. A few services cancel with no confirmation step at all. We know which ones and skip them.

How does it get into my accounts?

It doesn't. It runs in your own browser, on the services you're already signed into, and clicks through them the way you would. We have no login details, we never see a password, and it can't do anything on a service you're not signed into.

What's a leaving price?

The offer a company makes when you go to cancel: a discount, or a few free months, to keep you. Most people never see it because they never start cancelling. Walkaway starts, gets it, and stops.

Which services work?

Any that make an offer when you go to leave. Streaming, news, music, software, VPNs, phone plans, fitness apps. Some won't make one, and we leave those alone.

What if there's no offer?

You pay nothing. Your card is saved at checkout but never charged.

Can I scan more than once?

As often as you like, and scanning is free. Offers run for a set term, and most services won't offer again until it ends, so running again a few months later is the usual rhythm.

How is the fee worked out?

10% of the verified savings over each offer's term, added up, $1 minimum. Say $24 a month drops to $7 for six months. That's $102 saved, so the fee is $10.20. If some of the services you picked don't come through, the charge is lower. You only pay on what was verified.

What does the extension look at?

First, which sites you're signed into. Only their names are sent, to work out which are subscriptions. Then, for each subscription, the text of its account page and of the cancellation pages it goes through is sent to our AI, so it can read your price and find the offer. It only touches the tab it's working in. Cookies, passwords and your history never leave your browser.
